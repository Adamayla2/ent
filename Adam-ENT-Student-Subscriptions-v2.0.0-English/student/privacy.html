Adam ENT Master Bank - Privacy Policy

Version 2.0.0 — 24 October 2026.

The account service collects your name, email address, password, a random app installation identifier, and device name. Passwords are stored on the server as hashes with a separate salt for each password. The installation identifier does not read your phone number or device serial number.

This information is used to sign you in, manage your devices, verify your subscription, and authorise question bank downloads. Subscription order details, amounts, currency, and payment status are stored. When live payments are enabled, the payment provider processes card details on its own checkout page. The student app does not collect card numbers or security codes. Sandbox payments are simulated and do not charge money.

Questions, answers, notes, bookmarks, and exam history are stored locally in a separate space for each account. This version does not synchronise study progress with the server. Signing out or reaching subscription expiry does not erase progress, but clearing app data or uninstalling the app may remove it.

An internet connection is required to sign in, download questions, and renew verification. A signed licence permits offline study for up to seven days after successful verification, or until subscription expiry if earlier. A previously issued offline licence may remain valid until its expiry after the device is revoked from another device.

This version contains no advertising, tracking tools, or third-party analytics. Hosting and payment providers may retain operational and transaction records under their own policies.

Account deletion

You can delete your account from the Account screen after confirming your password, or from the website account deletion page. Account details, orders, devices, and sessions are deleted from the service database, and the app requests removal of that account's local data in the current browser. This does not remotely erase data on other devices or records held by an independent payment provider. The service operator must define its backup and hosting log retention policy before launch.

For questions or account deletion requests through support, use the email address below. Do not send your password or card details.

This app is an educational revision tool and does not provide patient diagnosis or treatment.
